PERSISTENCE
a computed dream, after Dalí

plate i · cap de creus

The desert dreams.

A hundred thousand grains of light settle into a plain that has never existed, under a sun that never sets. Everything you will see is computed, this instant, by the machine in your hands — hand-calculated dream photographs. An egg hangs where the horizon should be. Scroll.

plate ii · la montre molle

Time goes soft.

The clock keeps perfect time and cannot hold its shape — it sags, drips, gathers itself, and sags again, forever. Bring your cursor close: wherever you touch, the hour melts faster.

plate iii · les éléphants

The elephants pass.

On legs a hundred times too thin they carry their own weight lightly, the way impossible things do. Their shadows are longer than the day. They notice your cursor and would rather keep their distance.

plate iv · cygnes et éléphants

The water lies.

Two swans ride a bay that is perfectly still, and the bay disagrees: their reflection comes back as elephants. Neither is wrong. Dalí painted the trick in 1937 — here the mirror computes it, grain for grain, from the same points of light that make the swans.

plate v · méthode paranoïaque-critique

It is two things.

Dalí trained himself to see one form as another and called it the paranoiac-critical method. Here it is, computed: drag left and right. Face on, it is a word. Turned, the same points of light insist they were always a clock.

plate vi · galatea de las esferas

She is made of spheres.

Matter is mostly emptiness arranged with conviction. The dome hangs together out of habit, sphere by sphere. Move — it leans toward your hand. Click — it remembers being struck, then forgives.

colophon

Painted with
arithmetic.

No images, no video, no libraries. Every soft watch and stilted elephant is a procedural shape; the melting is a force field; the swans' lying reflection is the honest mirror of each grain, bent toward a second shape; the double image is the same hundred thousand particles seen from a different angle. The dream adapts its own fidelity to your hardware while you read this.
